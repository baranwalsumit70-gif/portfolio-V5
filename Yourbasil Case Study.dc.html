01 · Overview

The brief: raise Average Order Value from the product page.

Basil sells lunch boxes, water bottles, food jars, school bags, plates, pickle and dip containers and gift products for kids. 70% of customers on yourbasil.com purchase only one product per order, which directly impacts AOV. The task: using the product detail page as the canvas, design at least one section that upsells or cross-sells, and explain why this section, why this placement and which behaviour or CRO principle it is built on.

03 · Audit

The page sells one product well, and nothing more.

The current page has price, a vibe picker, a personalisation toggle and Add to cart. But nothing on the page pushes a second item, and the offers that would reward it sit in the wrong place.

04 · Key insight

The discount logic lives in the cart, not the product page. Customers only discover it after they've added one item. Too late.

05 · The redesign

Move the offer to the moment of decision.

Bring the offer logic onto the product page and place every cross-sell where buying momentum is highest: right around Add to cart.

What I learned

Placement mattered more than the component. The same offers already existed in the cart; moving them to the moment of decision, and letting every item count toward them, is what turns a one-item visit into a kit.
