09 · Learnings

What I learned.

01Explain every scoreA number alone was not enough. Showing the reason behind each point is what made the score usable.
02Lead with the next actionEvery screen ends in a decision: shortlist, screen or reject. Reading comes second.
03Build with the devs' systemDesigning on shadcn/ui kept design and code in sync and cut handoff time.
